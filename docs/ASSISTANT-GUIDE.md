# Putting a meeting on the iPad, and clearing it afterwards

For whoever prepares the iPads. No technical knowledge needed. Everything works with no internet.

## What you need

- A USB flash drive or an SD card (with a USB-C card reader). Any ordinary one.
- The files for the meeting.

## Step 1: Get the files ready on your computer (5 minutes)

1. Make a folder on your computer and name it for the meeting, for example **Board Meeting Dec 8**.
2. Put the files in it:
   - **PDFs, photos and videos** can go in as they are.
   - **PowerPoint, Word or Excel files:** open them and choose **File, Save As, PDF** (or **Export, PDF**), then put the PDF in the folder. The iPad cannot open the original files.
   - Videos should be **MP4**. If one will not play, ask IT.
   - Name each file the way the executive should see it, for example **Q4 Board Book** rather than **final_v7_FINAL**.
3. Copy the folder onto the USB drive or SD card.

If IT gave you the "Prep" tool, it does steps 2 and 3 for you and also writes the agenda.

## Step 2: Put it on the iPad (2 minutes)

1. Plug the USB drive (or SD card reader) into the iPad.
2. In the app, tap **Manage** (top right). If it asks for a PIN, type it.
3. Tap **Choose a folder**, find your USB drive in the list, tap your meeting folder, then tap **Open**.
   - Or tap **Choose files** to pick individual files. The iPad then asks **which meeting** they belong to: pick **New meeting** and type its name, or **A meeting already here**.
4. Wait for **Added**. If some files could not be added, it tells you which and why.
5. Tap **Done**. The meeting appears on the main screen. You can unplug the drive.

Check it: open each document once to make sure it looks right.

## Step 3: After the meeting, clear the iPad (30 seconds)

1. Tap **Manage**.
2. Tap **Clear everything for the next meeting**, then tap **Remove everything**.
3. The iPad says it is empty and ready.

To remove just one meeting, tap **Remove** next to its name instead.
To remove all meetings that have already happened, tap **Remove finished meetings** (it only appears when there are some).

Clearing removes the documents, photos, videos, any notes typed during the meeting, and any handwriting done with the Pencil. **If someone needs their notes, ask IT to copy them off before you clear.**

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The USB drive is not in the list | Unplug it, plug it in again, tap **Choose files** again. Try the other USB port. |
| "Not added, because this app cannot open them" | Those files are in a format the iPad cannot show. Save them as PDF (see Step 1). |
| "Could not be copied" | The drive was unplugged during copying, or the iPad is full. Check **Free space** at the bottom of **Manage**, clear old meetings, and try again. |
| Forgot the PIN | Ask IT. The iPad has to be reset from the Mac. |
| A document will not open | It may be password protected. Remove the password and add it again. |

## Good habits

- Load content the day before, then open each document once.
- Keep the PIN written down somewhere safe, not on the iPad.
- Clear the iPad after every meeting. Confidential material should not stay on it.
