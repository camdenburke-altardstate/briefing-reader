# Your iPad: quick guide

One page. Everything works with no internet.

## Reading

| You want to | Do this |
|---|---|
| Open something | Tap it on the main screen. Meetings are listed with their agenda; tap an agenda line to open that document. |
| Turn the page | Tap the **left or right edge**, or **swipe**. In landscape, a report opens like a book, two pages at a time. |
| Show the menu | Tap the **middle** of the page. It hides itself when you turn a page. |
| Jump to a page | Show the menu and **drag the slider**. A small picture of the page appears. Let go to jump. |
| Find a word in this document | Menu, **magnifier**. |
| Find a word in everything | On the main screen, **magnifier** (top right). |
| Chapters and your bookmarks | Menu, **list** button. |
| Mark a page to come back to | Menu, **bookmark** button. A ribbon appears on the page. |
| Zoom | Pinch. Double-tap on a photo. |
| Go back to the main screen | Menu, **arrow** at the top left. |

## Writing and notes

- **Write on a page with the Apple Pencil:** menu, **pencil** button. Pick a pen, highlighter or eraser. Tap
  **Done writing** when finished. Your handwriting is saved beside the document, never inside it.
- **Type meeting notes:** **Notes** on the main screen, or the **pencil-on-paper** button inside a document.
  **Add time** stamps a line.

## Screen

- **Sun button:** brightness, and for documents a **White, Warm or Night** page.
- The **time and battery** are shown in the corner. Charge the iPad before a long meeting.

## Good to know

- The iPad is set up to show only this app. That is on purpose, to keep the material safe.
- Nothing you do here goes online. Screenshots are turned off.
- Your handwriting and notes stay on the iPad. IT can copy them off if you ask.
- Documents are loaded and cleared by your assistant before and after each meeting.

## If something is wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| A document is missing | Ask whoever loads the iPad (see the assistant guide). |
| The screen is dark | Press the top button, or raise the brightness with the sun button. |
| The iPad is frozen | Hold the top button and the volume button together for 10 seconds, then release. |
| Anything else | Contact: __________________ (fill in IT's name and number) |
